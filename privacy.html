ViralClip AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ViralClip AI is a desktop tool run by an independent creator. It edits highlights from livestreams (used only with the streamer's permission) into short vertical clips and publishes them to the ViralClip AI accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Data we access

  • Our own social accounts. When the account owner authorizes ViralClip AI through the official login of each platform (Google OAuth, TikTok Login Kit, Instagram Login), the app receives access tokens limited to the requested permissions: basic profile info, uploading/publishing videos, and reading video statistics.
  • Public livestream chat. To find interesting moments, the app reads public chat messages of livestreams from creators who authorized us. Messages are analyzed for activity peaks and are not sold or shared.

How data is used and stored

  • Tokens and data are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used only to publish our clips and to measure their performance.
  • We do not sell personal data, show ads, or share data with third parties, except the platforms themselves when publishing.
  • Chat logs and raw recordings are deleted automatically after a limited period.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the security settings of the connected account (Google: myaccount.google.com → Third-party connections; TikTok and Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites). Creators who want their content removed can contact us and we will delete it.
