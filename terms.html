ViralClip AI - Terms of Service

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ViralClip AI is a private desktop tool used by its operator to edit livestream highlights into short vertical clips and to publish them on the operator's own ViralClip AI accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Use of content

  • Clips are made only from streams whose creators gave permission, or under the rules of campaigns that grant that permission.
  • Every clip credits the original creator and channel.
  • Creators may ask for any clip to be removed at any time, and it will be removed.

Platform rules

ViralClip AI uses only the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch and follows their terms, community guidelines and publishing limits. Nothing is published without the operator's review.
